Our Founder

Jacob Bagyadoss

Jacob Bagyadoss is an entrepreneur, business mentor, and community leader with a passion for developing people and building Kingdom-minded businesses.

As the Founder and Chairman of Jacobs Group of Companies, he oversees diverse business activities across multiple key sectors. Through TKL, Jacob leverages his decades of entrepreneurial experience to mentor local business owners, student cohorts, and civic coordinators.

Founder's Mission Statement

"To raise kingdom-driven leaders across Administration, business, politics, and culture through practical empowerment, civil education, and a commitment to societal excellence, nurturing ethical business owners, students, professionals, and social leaders to transform society through principled growth, civil services coaching, and community nation-building."
